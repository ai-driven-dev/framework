# Measurement may reach a hosted destination, and the destination is never ours to fix

Read before sending measurement off the machine, or growing what the local report answers.

## Privacy stands

- Measurement is off until a project opts in; `AIDD_TELEMETRY=0` in a person's environment refuses whatever the repository says.
- No code, prompt or diff is ever stored.
- A destination changes where measurement may go, never what it may contain.

## Transport

- No single standard transport: the shipped route reads the files each AI tool already wrote.
- OpenTelemetry is one shape a destination may speak, not the framework's own.
- The sink is local: JSON Lines under the person's profile ([usage contract](../../../product/usage-contract.md)).
- Nothing ships that opens a port or sends a record.

## Boundary

- The framework records, exposes a documented record and answers a few direct questions through its skills.
- Aggregation across people or repositories, saved views and anything pushed to someone belong to a destination.
- Local does not grow into an analytics product; a new report axis needs a reason a single machine must answer it.
- The record contract and the sink's shape are the destination's interface, versioned with an API's care.

## The destination is never ours to fix

- The person names the destination in configuration; no host is compiled in, defaulted or preferred.
- What travels is the published record contract, never a payload shaped for one vendor.
- A self-run destination reaches the same result as a paid one.
- Measuring and reading need no account; sending is a separate opt-in on top of measurement's.
- Authentication belongs to the destination; its absence is not a degraded mode.
- A person reads what a destination holds about them at least as early as anyone else, and sees what was sent.

## Rejected

- Keep "no SaaS": every cross-machine feature becomes unbuildable, and a hosted product would fork the framework.
- Bless one hosted product: the framework becomes its client, and a self-run destination becomes second class.
