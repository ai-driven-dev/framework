# Sources

Use each source only for what it can support.

| Source | Typical items | Can support | Cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ticketing tool | issues, requests, comments | recurring asks and their volume | why users ask, or who never asks |
| Support desk | tickets, chats | pain and friction as reported | the silent majority |
| Team wiki | meeting notes, research pages | what the team recorded | what customers said verbatim, unless quoted |
| Email | customer threads | first-hand statements | frequency across the base |
| Team chat | channel messages, forwarded feedback | fresh signals and reported quotes | first-hand wording, when relayed |
| Meeting transcripts | sales, success, or discovery calls | exact customer words in context | calls that were not recorded |
| Interview syntheses | themes and quotes from user interviews | depth on a few voices | prevalence |
| Reviews and surveys | app store reviews, NPS or CSAT verbatims | public or scored sentiment | intent of non-respondents |

## Access

Try each route in order and record the one used.

| Order | Route | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | a connected tool, found by the capability it offers | tool, query, read date |
| 2 | the ticketing tool configured in project memory, then repo configuration, then environment | tool, query, read date |
| 3 | an export file the PM provides | file name, export date |
| 4 | content the PM pastes | who pasted it, paste date |
| 5 | none reachable | a coverage gap |

| Case | Handling |
| --- | --- |
| Results are paginated or capped | follow every page, or record the source as incomplete |
| A source needs rights the PM lacks | record the gap and name who holds access |
| Items hold personal data | keep the account or role as the voice; copy no email, phone, or address |
| A relayed quote has no first-hand source | count it as a comment, never as a verbatim |
