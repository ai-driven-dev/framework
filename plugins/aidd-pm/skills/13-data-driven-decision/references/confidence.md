# Confidence

Default thresholds. The tracking plan may change them before collection, never after.

| Level | All of | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | 20 or more distinct voices, 3 or more source kinds, full window read, spot-check at least 8 of 10 | the signal is consistent across sources |
| Medium | 10 or more distinct voices, 2 or more source kinds | the signal is real; its size is uncertain |
| Low | 5 or more distinct voices | a lead worth checking, not a basis to decide |
| Insufficient | anything less, or one source holding the whole count | too thin to conclude |

## Gaps

Name every gap that applies, per topic.

| Gap | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Source not reached | no access to the support desk |
| Partial read | results capped at the first 100 items |
| Missing population | churned accounts never interviewed |
| Selection bias | support tickets only capture problems |
| Relayed only | every comment comes second-hand through the sales team |
| Ambiguous definition | spot-check agreement below 8 of 10 |
| Short window | fewer buckets than needed to read a trend |
