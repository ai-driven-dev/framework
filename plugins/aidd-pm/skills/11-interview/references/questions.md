# Questions

A good question makes the person tell a past story, never predict, approve, or design.

## Checks

| Check | Pass when | Instead of | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open | it cannot be answered yes or no | "Do you track this?" | "How do you track this today?" |
| Non-leading | it names no expected answer, product, or solution | "Would a dashboard help?" | "What did you do next?" |
| Past-anchored | it targets a specific past event, not future intent | "Would you use it weekly?" | "When did this last happen?" |
| Single | it asks one thing | "How and why do you do it?" | "How do you do it?" then "Why?" |
| Neutral | it carries no judgment word | "What is frustrating about it?" | "How did that go?" |

## Sequence

| Section | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Opening | purpose of the meeting, consent to record, no product shown |
| Context | the person's role, routine, and where the topic sits in it |
| Last time | walk through the last concrete occurrence, step by step |
| Pains and workarounds | what went wrong, what they did instead, what it cost |
| Hypothesis probes | questions that can refute each hypothesis |
| Wrap-up | what was missed, who else to talk to, follow-up agreed |

## Follow-ups

- "Tell me more about that."
- "Why was that important?"
- "Can you show me?"
- "What happened just before?"
- A silence of a few seconds.

## What to observe

| Signal | Tells |
| --- | --- |
| Emotion: a sigh, a laugh, a change of pace | where a pain is real |
| A workaround, file, or tool shown | the need is already paid for |
| Said and done diverge | which claim to trust less |
| A number volunteered | the size of the problem |
| The person's own words for things | the vocabulary to reuse |
| A topic avoided or cut short | a question to revisit |
