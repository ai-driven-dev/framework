# Insights

An insight restates in neutral words what the verbatims show. It is evidence, not a decision.

## Kinds

| Kind | Is | Cites |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pain | a problem the person lived, with its consequence or workaround | at least one verbatim |
| Need | a progress the person seeks, stated or shown | at least one verbatim |
| Open question | what the meeting left unresolved, vague, or contradictory | the verbatim or question that raised it |

## Rules

| Rule | Handling |
| --- | --- |
| Interpretation beyond the words | labeled `interpretation`, kept apart from the insight |
| One interview | never a frequency, share, or trend claim |
| Hypothesis from the guide | marked `supports`, `challenges`, or `no signal`, with verbatim ids; never `validated` |
| Figure | only as said in the transcript, with its verbatim id |

## Themes

Themes let rows from several interviews be counted together later.

| Rule | Handling |
| --- | --- |
| Form | kebab-case noun phrase, such as `onboarding-friction` |
| Vocabulary | reuse the user's theme list when one exists; otherwise propose one |
| Count | one theme per insight, a second only when both are needed |
| Row | id, kind, theme, statement, verbatim ids, speaker role |
