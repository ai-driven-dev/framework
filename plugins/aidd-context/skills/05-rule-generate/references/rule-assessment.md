# Rule assessment

How a candidate compares with each found policy, and what to offer.

| Label | Means | Offer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| new | no policy covers, repeats or contradicts it | create, cancel |
| covered | existing policies already require all of it in its scope | cancel |
| overlap | a policy repeats or extends part of it, compatibly | create, merge into it, cancel |
| conflict | a policy requires the opposite, or the candidate weakens one | rescope the candidate, cancel |
