# Google Antigravity

- Current conversation (CLI or app). `/schedule` accepts a delay or five-field cron and a prompt. Inspect the timer in the CLI's `/tasks` panel or the app conversation. [Session schedules](https://antigravity.google/docs/commands/schedule/)
- Persistent app task. Give `/automation` the prompt, cadence and timezone. The task starts paused. Review its permissions and state in Automations. [Background automations](https://antigravity.google/docs/commands/automation/)
