# 02 - Propose

Propose at least two variants of the screen of one wireframe.

## Input

The wireframes to dress, and the engine.

## Process

1. Take the next wireframe: its record, its render, its components.
2. Draw at least two variants from its zones and states.
   - Follow [variants](../references/variants.md).
3. Write each variant as a screen.
   - Follow [screens](../references/screens.md).
   - Use the [screen](../assets/screen-template.md) template.
   - Use the [render](../assets/render-template.html) template.
4. Compare the set per [engine](../references/engine.md).
   - Fix what it reports, and compare again until it accepts.
5. Read [choose](03-choose.md).

## Output

The set of variants, compared.
