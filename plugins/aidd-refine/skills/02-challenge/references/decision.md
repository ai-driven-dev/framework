# Decision

Ask these in order, and the first yes classifies the finding.

| # | Question | Yes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Does the finding need an input no user enters and no attacker gains from, such as a quantity beyond any stock or a number past a type's limit? | Dropped |
| 2 | Does the work break something it was asked to do, shown by a command and its output, or by its own lines? | Deal breaker |
| 3 | Does the work do what was asked, while a case its users hit defeats the aim? | To decide |
| 4 | Would it make the work serve its aim better? | Suggestion |
| 5 | None of the above. | Dropped |
