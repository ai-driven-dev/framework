# Lenses

Where a source usually leaves a builder guessing.

| Element | Spot it as | Ask it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actor | a person, role, team or system that acts or is affected | Who triggers it, who decides, who is told? |
| Action | a verb the product or a user performs | Who performs it? What happens when it fails, times out, or runs twice? |
| Data | a field, record, file or message | Where does it come from, who may read or change it, what when it is missing or malformed? |
| Term | an adjective, a metric word, a domain word | Could two builders read it two ways and build different things? |
| Bound | a number, limit, list, date or duration | What happens at zero, at the limit, past it, when empty, when duplicated? |
| External | a service, API, library, data set or prior step | Is it named, who owns it, what when it is down or slow? |
| Requirement | a must, shall, should or will | How does a tester prove it passes or fails? |
| Premise | a condition the sentence needs to hold | What must already be true, and is it written anywhere? |

- A question the source answers elsewhere is no gap.
