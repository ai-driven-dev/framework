# 02 - Explore

Clarify the idea through questions until it is clear enough to conclude.

## Input

The conversation so far.

## Process

1. Take stock of what is clear, what is assumed and why, and what is open.
2. Pick the next point that could still change the outcome: what the last message opened, or the next one in the [discovery map](../references/discovery-map.md).
   - No point left: go to step 5.
3. Ask about it with the [probing techniques](../references/probing.md) that fit.
4. Wait for the answer, then repeat from step 1.
5. Go to [conclude](03-conclude.md).

## Output

The idea clear enough to conclude.

## Rules

- On a contradiction, ask which one stands.
- When facts converge, state your leaning and its tradeoff.
- Stay at the user's level and leave the how to planning.
- Sound like a conversation, not a form.
