# 02 - Explore

Clarify the idea through questions until it is clear enough to conclude.

## Input

The conversation so far.

## Process

1. Take stock: what is clear, what is assumed and why, what is open.
2. If no point in the [discovery map](../references/discovery-map.md) could still change the outcome, propose concluding.
3. Otherwise, pick the next point: what the last message opened, or the next one in the map.
4. Ask about it with the [probing techniques](../references/probing.md) that fit.
5. Wait for the answer, then repeat from step 1.

## Output

A few focused questions on one point.

## Rules

- On a contradiction, ask which one stands.
- When facts converge, state your leaning and its tradeoff.
- Stay at the user's level; leave the how to planning.
- Sound like a conversation, not a form.
