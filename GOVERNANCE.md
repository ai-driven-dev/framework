# Governance

How decisions are made in the AI-Driven Dev Framework.

## Roles

Four roles form a ladder. Each one keeps the rights of the roles below it.

| Role | Who | Adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Public** | Anyone with a GitHub account | Open issues, comment, react to ideas, and open a pull request once its issue is validated |
| **Core Team** | An active member of the [AIDD programme](https://www.ai-driven-dev.fr/) | A counted vote on the roadmap |
| **Certified Member** | Someone who passed the [AIDD certification](https://www.ai-driven-dev.fr/) | Validate contribution issues |
| **Maintainer** | A certified member who gives AIDD trainings, or AIDD staff | Approve and merge pull requests, block a change that falls short, promote people |

## Code decisions

Only a Maintainer merges. By default:

- A Maintainer may merge after one Maintainer approval, passing checks, and 72 hours with no objection from another Maintainer.
- Any Maintainer can block a change by requesting changes, until it is resolved.
- A change across plugins, to a contract, to the license or to this document needs two Maintainer approvals.

## The roadmap

The live roadmap is the [AIDD Roadmap board](https://github.com/orgs/ai-driven-dev/projects/8). A milestone is a theme due on a Thursday. Items without a milestone are the backlog, ordered by priority.

Anyone can react to an idea. A reaction is a signal, not a vote: it moves the idea to a formal vote. Core Team members, Certified Members and Maintainers each cast one equal vote. A poll runs at least 7 days, and a Maintainer breaks ties. This vote sets the roadmap order. It does not decide whether a validated issue can become a pull request.

## How to influence the roadmap

- Join the weekly Discord event [Framework Evolution](https://discord.gg/DyzRuZ7SF?event=1514565536695193710), where the roadmap is decided.
- Open an issue or an idea in [Discussions](https://github.com/ai-driven-dev/framework/discussions), and 👍 what you want.
- Talk in the [framework channel on Discord](https://discord.com/channels/1173363373115723796/1512081578858119238).
- To fund work, see [`FUNDING.yml`](.github/FUNDING.yml).

Shipped changes are in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).
